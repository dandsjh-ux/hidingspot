    How to play

    Hider. Find cover before the countdown ends. Stand behind a wall, crouch beside a hedge or go prone under a low stall. Stay hidden until time runs out.

    Seekers. Walk up to a villager and ask for a clue. Follow the directions and look at the map when a search circle appears. Get close to the hider, look at them and tag them.

    Keyboard. Use WASD or arrow keys and look with the mouse. Shift runs, C crouches and Z goes prone. Press E to ask or tag, M for the map, B for binoculars, F for the drone and P for the menu. Esc releases the mouse.

    Tablet. Drag the left stick to move. Drag the town view to look around. Tap the stance, equipment and action buttons. Landscape gives you a wider view.

    Drone. Use your movement controls to fly. The camera can spot the hider from above if the view is clear. Tap Drone again or press F to land.

    Word help

    near / beside
    Close to something. “I hid beside the fountain.”
    behind
    At the back of something. “She is behind the hedge.”
    under
    Below something. “I crawled under the stall.”
    across
    From one side to the other. “Walk across the bridge.”
    north / east / south / west
    Directions. On the town map, north is up and east is right.
    striped
    With bands of colour. “Look near the striped stall.”
    shady
    Out of the sunlight. “They went past the shady trees.”
    shutters
    Covers on the sides of a window.
    prone
    Lying on your front, close to the ground.
